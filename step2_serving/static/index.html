Face Recognition Demo

PyTorch models served with FastAPI on Google Cloud Run

Upload a photo with people in it. Faces are detected and aligned with MTCNN, embedded with FaceNet, and matched against 158 public figures from the LFW dataset. Anyone else is labeled Unknown.

Who can it recognize?

    Upload a photo and a ResNet-50 trained on ImageNet will guess what's in it, out of 1000 categories.

    Photos are processed in memory and never stored.

    Source code: github.com/QQ1033/face-recognition-from-scratch
